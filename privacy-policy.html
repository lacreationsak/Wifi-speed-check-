Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Signal. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you use our internet connection and speed testing website.

We designed Signal to provide connection measurements while keeping the amount of personal information collected to a minimum.

2. Information Used During Testing

When you perform a connection test, Signal may process technical information required to perform and display the test, including:

  • Download speed
  • Upload speed
  • Ping / latency
  • Jitter
  • Packet loss, where available
  • Connection type
  • Selected test server
  • Signal strength information, where available

3. Test History

Signal may display your previous test results so that you can compare connection performance over time.

Where test history is stored locally by the website, it remains in your browser/device unless you clear the stored browser data.

4. Location Information

Some connection-related features may use approximate network or location information when required to identify suitable testing conditions or servers.

Signal should request browser permission before accessing precise device location. You can deny this permission and continue using features that do not require location access.

5. Network Information

Depending on your browser and network, Signal may be able to display technical connection information such as connection type, network-related information, or internet service provider information.

Browser capabilities can limit the information available to Signal. Signal does not claim to have access to information that your browser does not expose.

6. Gaming Test

If you use the Gaming Test, Signal may measure connection characteristics relevant to online gaming, such as latency, jitter and packet loss.

These measurements are used to provide the gaming connection result displayed by the website.

7. Feedback

If you submit feedback through Signal, the information you voluntarily provide may be received and processed for the purpose of understanding user experience and improving the website.

Please do not include passwords, payment information, private credentials, or other sensitive personal information in feedback.

8. Cookies and Local Storage

Signal may use browser storage technologies, including local storage, where necessary to remember preferences or display recent test results.

If you clear your browser's stored website data, locally stored Signal information may also be removed.

9. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information processed through Signal. However, no website or internet transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. Third-Party Services

Signal may use third-party infrastructure or testing services to perform network measurements. Information required for a connection test may therefore be transmitted to the relevant service.

The handling of information by those services may be subject to their own privacy policies.

11. Children's Privacy

Signal is not designed to intentionally collect personal information from children. Users should avoid submitting unnecessary personal information through the feedback system.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the website, features, or data practices change. The updated version will be published on this page with a revised update date.

13. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how Signal handles information, please contact the website owner through the contact method provided on the website.
